# Re-Hardwire — Long-Term Roadmap (Weeks 6 to 15)

Building upon our foundational 5-week rollout, this long-term progression roadmap outlines features, architectural expansions, and ecosystem milestones from **Week 6 through Week 15**.

---

## Week 6: Advanced Multimodal Voice & Speech Dictation
- **Web Speech API Integration**: Full microphone dictation support in the chat composer and journaling surfaces.
- **Voice-Guided Breathing Exercises**: Audio-paced breathing circles with haptic and spoken prompts.

---

## Week 7: Longitudinal Mood & Resilience Analytics
- **Trend Visualizations**: Interactive charts and weekly heatmaps tracking emotional states, signal velocity, and micro-win streaks.
- **Progress Insights**: Automated weekly summaries highlighting coping momentum and successful protocols.

---

## Week 8: Custom Coping Practice Builder
- **User-Authored Tools**: Ability for users to create, tag, and save custom grounding or breathing practices tailored to their personal toolkit.
- **Local Practice Packs**: Export and import custom practice definitions across devices.

---

## Week 9: End-to-End Encrypted Cloud Sync (Zero-Knowledge)
- **Zero-Knowledge Encryption**: Client-side AES-GCM encryption of support plans and care networks before optional server backup.
- **Multi-Device Pairing**: Secure cryptographic pairing between a user's phone, tablet, and web browser.

---

## Week 10: Biometric Lock & Privacy Vault
- **Device Security Integration**: Optional fingerprint (BiometricPrompt) or PIN lock required to open sensitive support plans and relationship boundary settings.
- **Stealth / Privacy Mode**: Quick blur or hide toggle for notification banners and screen switchers.

---

## Week 11: Standalone Wear OS App & Complications
- **Standalone Watch App**: Full Wear OS Compose Material 3 watch application running independent grounding pulses and breath timers.
- **Interactive Watch Complications**: Live protocol status and next-action reminders right on the watch face.

---

## Week 12: Comprehensive Multi-Language Localization
- **Localized UI & Content**: Full translations for Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Portuguese across all guided tools and support plan templates.
- **Dialect Voice Selection**: Automatic matching of text-to-speech voices to the user's localized dialect.

---

## Week 13: Clinical Collaboration & Therapist Export
- **Therapist-Friendly PDF Summary**: Clean, printable, and structured export format designed specifically for sharing coping strategies with a clinician or counselor.
- **Secure Review Notes**: Private journaling space for therapy sessions and professional check-ins.

---

## Week 14: System-Level AppFunctions & AI Assistant Integration
- **Android AppFunctions (`androidx.appfunctions`)**: Exposing Re-Hardwire grounding practices and support plans as system-level shortcuts for AI assistants.
- **Voice Assistant Hand-Off**: Launching breathing exercises directly via voice commands ("Hey Google, start a breathing exercise in Re-Hardwire").

---

## Week 15: Version 2.0 Global Release & Open-Source Handover
- **Public Stable 2.0 Rollout**: Google Play Store and Apple App Store production release of Version 2.0.
- **Community Governance & Documentation**: Finalizing open-source contributor guidelines, developer API specs, and clinical safety whitepapers.
