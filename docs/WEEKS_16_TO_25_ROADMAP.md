# Re-Hardwire — Long-Term Ecosystem Roadmap (Weeks 16 to 25)

Expanding beyond our Version 2.0 foundation, this roadmap outlines advanced biofeedback, spatial grounding, federated privacy, clinical EHR interoperability, and system-level automation from **Week 16 through Week 25**.

---

## Week 16: Spatial Computing & Immersive WebXR Grounding
- **Spatial 3D Grounding Environments**: WebXR-based 3D calm environments for headset and projected display glasses (such as Android XR displays).
- **Spatial Audio Pacing**: 3D spatialized audio breathing queues that circle the user in 3D space.

---

## Week 17: Wearable HRV & Biofeedback Integration
- **Autonomic State Tracking**: Real-time Heart Rate Variability (HRV) monitoring over Bluetooth LE wearables to detect autonomic nervous system shifts.
- **Automated Down-Regulation Prompting**: Triggering Level 1 (`Stabilise & Breathe`) practices automatically when sudden HRV drops indicate stress spikes.

---

## Week 18: Federated Learning & On-Device Model Tuning
- **Privacy-Preserving On-Device Tuning**: Local model adaptation using federated learning principles where raw prompt history never leaves the user's hardware.
- **Differential Privacy Guarantees**: Mathematical privacy bounds for optional aggregated routing stats.

---

## Week 19: Peer Support Circles & Anonymous Group Check-Ins
- **Air-Gapped Group Dynamics**: Encrypted, local group check-ins for support groups or therapy cohorts.
- **Anonymous Micro-Win Sharing**: Opt-in encrypted sharing of logged wins within trusted peer circles.

---

## Week 20: Neuro-Linguistic & CBT Reframing Engine
- **Cognitive Restructuring Workflows**: Guided interactive worksheets for identifying cognitive distortions (e.g. catastrophizing, black-and-white thinking).
- **Adaptive Reframe Generation**: On-device AI assistance generating balanced alternative thoughts.

---

## Week 21: Cross-Platform Desktop & Large Screen Polish
- **Adaptive Multi-Pane Layouts**: Navigation 3 / Responsive grid layouts optimized for tablets, foldables, and desktop windows.
- **Power User Keyboard Shortcuts**: Custom shortcut keymaps (`Ctrl+K` command palette, `Ctrl+B` quick boundary toggle).

---

## Week 22: Offline On-Device Neural Speech Engine
- **On-Device Neural Voices**: Integrating offline high-quality neural TTS models for smooth, natural voice playback without network calls.
- **Custom Speaking Pace & Tone Controls**: Pitch and cadence adjustments tuned for anxiety reduction.

---

## Week 23: Clinical EHR & HL7 FHIR Interoperability
- **FHIR Standard Export**: Exporting support plan entries and self-reported coping metrics into standardized HL7 FHIR JSON objects for electronic health record (EHR) systems.
- **Clinician Consent Gate**: Strict double-confirmation privacy gate before generating clinical export payloads.

---

## Week 24: Smart Safety Automation & Autonomic Overload Detection
- **Wearable Overload Detection**: Smart fallbacks that notify designated care plan network contacts when severe, sustained stress overload is detected.
- **One-Tap Emergency Override**: Persistent single-touch crisis override that bypasses all app states.

---

## Week 25: Version 3.0 Global Launch & Clinical Whitepaper
- **Version 3.0 Enterprise Release**: Global distribution across Android, Wear OS, iOS, and WebXR platforms.
- **Peer-Reviewed Safety & Efficacy Whitepaper**: Publishing open-source clinical evaluation results on digital self-help grounding efficacy.
