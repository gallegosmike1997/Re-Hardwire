# Re-Hardwire — Week 5 Application Progression & Ecosystem Expansion

Following the successful completion of Week 4 (Release Candidate hardening, production signing, and Play Store bundle generation), **Week 5** focuses on post-release telemetry, Wear OS companion widgets, and on-device offline AI experimentation.

---

## 1. Crash Telemetry & Performance Monitoring
- **Stability Dashboards**: Tracking crash-free session rates and ANR (Application Not Responding) metrics across device form factors.
- **Battery & Memory Optimization**: Profiling background service usage and DOM memory footprint within the Capacitor WebView.

---

## 2. Wear OS Widget & Companion Integration
- **Glance Wear / Remote Compose**: Implementing lightweight watch face complications and glance widgets for quick grounding pulses and support plan access directly from the wrist.

---

## 3. On-Device Offline AI (LiteRT-LM Integration)
- **Zero-Latency Local LLM**: Exploring integration with on-device small language models (such as Gemma / LiteRT-LM) to provide fully private, offline AI coaching conversations directly on mobile hardware.
