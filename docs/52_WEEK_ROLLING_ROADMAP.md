# Re-Hardwire — 52-Week Rolling Roadmap

Building upon our completed 25-week foundation (offline-first architecture, 5-level protocol ladder, support plans, relationship boundaries, QR sync, WebXR spatial grounding, BLE HRV biofeedback, CBT reframing, and HL7 FHIR clinical export), this **52-Week Rolling Roadmap** outlines the strategic progression from **Week 26 through Week 52**.

---

## Quarter 3: Biofeedback, EEG & Neuro-Tech Integration (Weeks 26–30)
- **Week 26 — EEG Brainwave Analysis**: Alpha/Theta ratio parsing via Muse/OpenBCI Bluetooth LE sensors to measure deep relaxation depth.
- **Week 27 — Galvanic Skin Response (GSR)**: Real-time electrodermal activity tracking for sudden sympathetic arousal spikes.
- **Week 28 — Audio-Visual Entrainment**: Isochronic tone generators and visual pulse circles for brainwave entrainment.
- **Week 29 — Closed-Loop Biofeedback**: Automatically adjusting protocol intensity in real time based on active HRV and GSR inputs.
- **Week 30 — Neuro-Feedback Analytics**: Long-term stress recovery dashboards and autonomic resilience metrics.

---

## Quarter 4: Global Health Systems & Clinical EHR Interoperability (Weeks 31–38)
- **Week 31 — Epic & Cerner EHR Connectors**: Direct integration with major electronic health record platforms.
- **Week 32 — Automated HIPAA / GDPR Compliance**: Automated privacy audit logging and data boundary verification.
- **Week 33 — Clinical Trial Data Framework**: De-identified, consent-gated data collection for multi-center clinical trials.
- **Week 34 — Shared Clinical Action Plans**: Live co-editing of support plans between patients and licensed therapists.
- **Week 35 — Prescription Digital Therapeutics (PDT)**: Verification protocols for clinician-prescribed digital coping modules.
- **Week 36 — Risk Escalation Safeguards**: Automated, deterministic safety boundaries and crisis intervention hand-offs.
- **Week 37 — Enterprise Health System Admin Portal**: Multi-tenant organization dashboard for healthcare networks.
- **Week 38 — FDA SaMD Pre-Submission Packet**: Finalizing Software as a Medical Device regulatory documentation.

---

## Year 2 Q1: On-Device Agentic Swarms & Multimodal AI (Weeks 39–45)
- **Week 39 — Local Agent Swarm Orchestration**: On-device multi-agent loops via ML Kit GenAI Agent API and Gemini Nano.
- **Week 40 — Vocal Tone & Sentiment Analysis**: On-device audio signal processing to detect vocal tremors and speech rate acceleration.
- **Week 41 — Somatic Posture & Tension Detection**: On-device vision analysis for shoulder elevation and facial muscle tension.
- **Week 42 — Autonomous AppFunction Tool Calling**: System-level action execution without opening app UIs (`androidx.appfunctions`).
- **Week 43 — Ephemeral Privacy Memory Graphs**: Encrypted local graph memory for long-term personalized coaching context.
- **Week 44 — Clinical Supervisor Agent**: Safety supervisor agent monitoring agentic loops to prevent ungrounded advice.
- **Week 45 — Multimodal Pipeline Hardening**: Sub-100ms local inference pipeline optimization across CPU, GPU, and NPU backends.

## Year 2 Q2: Global Platform Scale & Open Ecosystem (Weeks 46–52)
- **Week 46 — Cross-Sensor Smart Wearables Mesh**: Unified sensor fusion across Apple Watch, Wear OS, and Galaxy Ring.
- **Week 47 — In-Vehicle Calm Mode (Android Auto)**: Parked-app calm practices and audio-guided breathing for drivers.
- **Week 48 — Offline BLE Mesh Peer Support**: Air-gapped local mesh network for offline group check-ins without cell service.
- **Week 49 — Universal Global Localization**: Full multi-language support across 30+ world languages and regional dialects.
- **Week 50 — Sovereign Cloud & On-Premise Hospital Deployments**: Air-gapped, containerized hospital deployments for strict data sovereignty.
- **Week 51 — Open-Source Developer SDK & Plugin Marketplace**: Public SDK for third-party clinical practice developers.

## Year 3 Q3: Decentralized Identity & Sovereign Data (Weeks 65–71)
- **Week 65 — Decentralized Identity (DID) Integration**: User-controlled digital identifiers via W3C DID standards, replacing phone/email-based logins.
- **Week 66 — Verifiable Credentials for Care Progress**: Zero-knowledge proof-of-progress certificates that users can share with clinicians without exposing raw data.
- **Week 67 — Edge-Compute Model Aggregation**: Periodic over-the-air updates to on-device ML models based on federated learning from the user cohort.
- **Week 68 — Offline-First Federated Learning**: Model improvement that works entirely offline, syncing aggregated updates when connectivity is available.
- **Week 69 — Patient-Generated Data as Research Input**: Opt-in aggregated, anonymized data contribution to approved digital mental health research studies.
- **Week 70 — SDK for Third-Party Clinical App Integration**: Toolkit for clinical developers to build interoperable apps that connect with Re-Hardwire protocol engine.
- **Week 71 — bidirectional FHIR Smarter APIs**: Two-way FHIR resource interactions for real-time support plan updates from EHR systems.
